Privacy policy

This policy explains what AutoPlay does with your information. AutoPlay is published by Nayan Suthar.

AutoPlay · Last updated October 18, 2026

How AutoPlay works

AutoPlay runs on your own computer. It has no server of its own: it talks to Google directly, and nothing it handles is sent to Nayan Suthar.

Information AutoPlay accesses

  • Your YouTube channel's basic details, such as its name and ID
  • Details of the videos on your channel, such as their titles and status
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails you choose to publish

Google permissions

When you connect your Google account, AutoPlay asks for these permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
    Upload the videos you choose to publish, with their titles and descriptions.
  • Manage your YouTube account
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube
    Read your channel's details and your videos' details, and set the thumbnail of each video AutoPlay uploads.

How it's shared

AutoPlay doesn't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. It sends information only to Google, to carry out what you ask it to do.

Where it's stored

When you connect a channel, Google issues a sign-in token. AutoPlay saves it in a file in its credentials folder on your computer, and sends it only to Google. Channel and video details are used while AutoPlay runs and aren't stored anywhere else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AutoPlay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

AutoPlay uses YouTube API Services. What YouTube does with your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting your data

You can revoke AutoPlay's access to your Google account at any time on your Google Account's security settings page. To remove the saved token too, delete the token file in AutoPlay's credentials folder on your computer.

Children

AutoPlay isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
