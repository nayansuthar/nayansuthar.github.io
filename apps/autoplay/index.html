AutoPlay

Turns SVG artwork into finished educational videos and publishes them to your YouTube channel.

AutoPlay is a video automation tool. It takes SVG artwork and animation clips, runs them through an automated pipeline to a finished timeline, and renders the result as a video.

When a video is ready, AutoPlay can publish it to a YouTube channel you've connected, with its title, description and thumbnail.

What it does

  • Build videos from SVG artwork and a library of animation clips
  • Automate every step, from the brief to the rendered video
  • Publish to one or more of your YouTube channels
