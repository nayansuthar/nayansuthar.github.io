Privacy policy

This policy explains what Workbench does with your information.

Workbench · Last updated October 18, 2026

How Workbench works

Workbench runs on the user's own computer. It has no server of its own: it talks to Google directly, and nothing it handles is sent anywhere else.

Information Workbench accesses

  • Your YouTube channel's basic details, such as its name and ID
  • Details of the videos on your channel, such as their titles and status
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails chosen for publishing

Google permissions

When you connect your Google account, Workbench asks for these permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
    Upload the videos chosen for publishing, with their titles and descriptions.
  • Manage your YouTube account
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube
    Read the channel's details and its videos' details, and set the thumbnail of each uploaded video.

How it's shared

Workbench doesn't sell information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. It sends information only to Google, to carry out what it's asked to do.

Where it's stored

When a Google account is connected, Google issues a sign-in token. Workbench saves it in a file on the user's computer and sends it only to Google. Channel and video details are used while a tool runs and aren't stored anywhere else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Workbench's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Workbench uses YouTube API Services. What YouTube does with your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting your data

You can revoke Workbench's access to your Google account at any time on your Google Account's security settings page. To remove the saved token too, delete the token file from Workbench's credentials folder.

Children

Workbench isn't meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
