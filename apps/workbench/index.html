Workbench

A personal app that connects the user's own tools and scripts to Google APIs.

Workbench is a general-purpose app for personal use. It signs in to Google on the user's behalf, so the tools and scripts on the user's computer can use Google APIs, such as publishing videos to YouTube.

It's meant for one person's own use: the only Google accounts it connects to are the user's own.

What it does

  • Signs in with Google, so no password is ever stored
  • Gives local tools and scripts access to the Google APIs they need
  • Publishes videos to YouTube, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails
